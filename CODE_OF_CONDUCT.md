# Code of conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues and pull requests
- Focus feedback on the code and ideas, not the person
- Accept that others may have different experience levels

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Deliberately disruptive or trolling behavior

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments and contributions that violate this code. Repeated violations may result in a ban from the project.
